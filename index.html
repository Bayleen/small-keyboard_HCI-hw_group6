Team 6 · HCI Bakeoff 3

Two keyboard designs.
One final direction.

A physically calibrated 1 × 1 inch smartwatch text-entry challenge. We explored a grouped flick keyboard and a magnified QWERTY keyboard, then refined each concept. Each version offers a 2-phrase Quick Test for homework practice and a 4-phrase Full Test using randomized official phrases.
