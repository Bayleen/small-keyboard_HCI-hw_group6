# Design 2 — Magnified QWERTY V2 (Second Concept)

**Previously:** `prototype-v4/`

A different keyboard design from StarFlick, emphasizing a familiar alphabet layout.

### Interaction

- **Bottom half:** full 26-letter QWERTY in three rows.
- **Top half while selecting:** a magnified local preview of nearby letters.
- **Touch, drag, release:** press a letter, adjust the highlighted letter while dragging, lift to enter it.
- **Top half when idle:** Delete on the left, cursor joystick in the center, Space on the right.
- The entire interactive keyboard fits within the ruler-calibrated **1 × 1 inch** square.

### Testing role

This is the **initial candidate for Design 2**, for comparing first-time discoverability and input performance against the grouped-flick approach.
