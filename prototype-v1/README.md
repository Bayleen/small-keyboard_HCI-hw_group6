# Design 1 — StarFlick V1 (First Prototype)

**Previously:** `prototype-v2/`

**Concept:** A nine-zone letter-group keyboard controlled by touch and directional flick. The enlarged radial selection dial offers letter feedback inside a physically calibrated one-inch square.

### Interaction

- Tap or flick within a group to select a letter.
- Three- and four-letter groups offer directional selection.
- Support space, delete, cancel and cursor editing within the keyboard region.
- The keyboard does not predict words or read the target phrase.

### Testing role

This is the **initial candidate for Design 1**. The team observed that learned flick patterns may be efficient for returning users; measured evidence should be recorded separately.
